Setting your markers in MS Word

  1. Open your manuscript in MS Word.
  2. Find the spot where your inciting incident happens. Type "Inciting Incident" or whatever label makes sense to you. I put mine in all caps and highlight it pink or blue so it's easy to spot later and delete once the manuscript is done.
  3. Select your marker. Open the References tab, then click Mark Citation. Give it a name if one doesn't pop up in the box on its own.
  4. Repeat those steps for Start of Act 2, Midpoint Reversal, Crisis, and Climax (use whatever terms work for you).
  5. After all your spots are marked, build the Table of Authorities by clicking Insert Table of Authorities. Put it at the very beginning or end of your document. It'll stay there until you finish.
  6. Any time you make big changes, refresh the table by clicking Update Table. Fair warning, this table is pretty ugly. It's only a temporary tool, but it lists every marker along with the page it sits on. Entries show up in alphabetical order. Apply your highlight colors before you mark a spot, or the color won't carry over into the table. Once you're happy with your pacing, delete the table and all your markers.
